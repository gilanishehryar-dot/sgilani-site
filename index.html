Point of view

Most capital isn't lost on bad projects. It's lost on good projects approved on numbers no one owned.

I've spent two decades on the decisions that carry the most capital: housing pipelines in the UK, giga-projects in Saudi Arabia, and master-planned communities today. The pattern is the same everywhere. The model is rarely the problem. The problem is that nobody can say who stands behind each assumption, where the downside sits, or what would change the answer.

My work fixes that. I bring finance, governance and strategy into one system, so Boards spend their time on judgement rather than on checking the arithmetic.
