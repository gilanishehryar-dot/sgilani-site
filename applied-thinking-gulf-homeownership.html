Applied thinking · Housing finance

A homeownership platform for the Gulf

Proven at scale in the UK. Rooted in Islamic finance. Built for the Gulf.

After 18 years at L&Q, one of the UK's largest shared ownership providers, I'm adapting the model to bring homeownership within reach for middle-income households in the Gulf.

The idea draws on two traditions. UK shared ownership lets a household buy a share of a home and increase it over time. Diminishing musharaka, a Sharia-compliant co-ownership structure, works the same way: the household's share grows as the investor's share falls. Combined into a registered platform, the two can build household wealth without subsidy while offering investors a governed, long-term return.
