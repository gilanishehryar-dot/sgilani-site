The assumptions register I use before any case goes to the Board

If no one owns the number, the Board is approving a guess. This one-page register gives every assumption an owner, a source, a tolerance and a rule for when it has to come back for a decision.

Download the Excel template

What it records

Every input that moves the investment case, tiered by how much it matters. Tier 1 assumptions are the ones where a reasonable move changes the decision. Tier 2 are supporting. Each has one named owner, one approver, the evidence behind it and the date it was last checked.

The part most registers miss is the tolerance. It sets how far the latest view can drift from the approved base before it must be escalated, and to whom. That turns the register from a list into a control.

What is in the workbook

  1. Register. Every input, tiered, with one named owner and one approver (a role, not a team), its source and an evidence grade.
  2. Two-level tolerances. Amber when an assumption drifts past the first limit, red when it crosses the second. Red items are routed to the right forum automatically.
  3. Review discipline. Review frequency is set by tier. Overdue reviews are flagged, so stale numbers can't sit quietly in a Board case.
  4. Dashboard and return bridge. A one-page view of red, amber and overdue items, a heat map, the five assumptions moving the return most, and a bridge from the approved return to today's indicative return.
  5. Change log, rules and ownership matrix. Every change is recorded with who approved it and whether they had the authority to. Escalation rules are agreed in advance.

Questions for your next Board meeting

Which five assumptions move our largest case the most, and who owns each of them? When did each last change, and did anyone tell us?
