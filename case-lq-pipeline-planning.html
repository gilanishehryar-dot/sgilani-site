Case study · Strategic Finance

Planning a £5bn+ pipeline for 110,000 homes

At one of the UK's largest housing associations, financial planning had to give the Board a clear view of funding, covenants and long-term capacity across a large development pipeline.

£5bn+development pipeline, 110,000 homes
OrganisationL&Q, one of the UK's largest housing associations
My roleDirector of Financial Planning and Performance
Team40+, reporting to the Group Finance Director

01 · The situation

A long-term pipeline, bounded by funding and covenants

A regulated housing association funding a large, long-term development pipeline. What could be built depended on funding capacity and covenant headroom as much as on demand.

02 · What I did

Led planning, performance and business partnering

  • Led financial planning, performance and business partnering with a team of 40+.
  • Reported to the Group Finance Director and supported the Board.
  • Improved forecasting processes and financial planning efficiency.
  • Developed the financial reporting and insight used for strategic decisions.

03 · The outcome

25% fasterForecasting cycle timeThrough improved forecasting processes and planning efficiency
FundingClear visibilityOf funding requirements and long-term financial capacity
CovenantsStronger forecastingCovenant monitoring that supported financial resilience
Board reportingSharper insightReporting that enabled better strategic decisions

What other Boards can take from this

In a regulated, debt-funded business, the plan is only as good as its view of capacity. Funding visibility and covenant headroom are what let a Board commit to a long-term pipeline with confidence.
